Electrical design & validation

Inverter & string sizing

Battery backup sizing

DC / AC protection & earthing screen

Preliminary equipment-rating calculations. Verify ratings, breaking capacity, coordination and applicable standards against manufacturer data.

SPD type and Uc/Ucpv require system earthing, lightning-risk and maximum PV-voltage studies. Final ratings require prospective fault-current and coordination checks.

Cable sizing & voltage-drop screen

Resistive voltage-drop estimate with conductor temperature correction. Ampacity, installation derating and short-circuit withstand are separate mandatory checks.

Preliminary resistive voltage-drop calculation only. Final cable selection must verify current-carrying capacity using installation method, ambient/soil temperature, grouping, thermal insulation, harmonics, protective-device coordination and applicable IS/IEC requirements.
